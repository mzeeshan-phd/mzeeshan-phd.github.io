    Research

    My research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, network traffic analysis and real-world attack scenarios. The aim is intrusion detection and prevention that is adaptive, efficient and deployable inside the network, and that moves beyond models tuned to a single dataset.

    Current Research Areas

    IoT, network and cloud security

    Intelligent intrusion detection for IoT networks, cloud environments and industrial systems. Recent and ongoing work includes attention-driven deep learning for DDoS detection in cloud networks, lightweight hybrid autoencoders for ARP-based man-in-the-middle detection, LSTM-based autoencoders for IoT botnet and Industrial IoT intrusion detection, DNS tunneling detection, and adversarially hardened Android malware detection. Published work in this area established protocol-based and feature-driven deep learning baselines on the UNSW-NB15 and BoT-IoT datasets and real-time SDN-based IDPS architectures.

    Phishing detection and URL classification

    Detection of web-based attacks that evade blacklists and signature filters, using feature engineering, deep learning, deep reinforcement learning and transformer-based hybrid models, including detection of synthetically generated phishing URLs and zero-content URL classification.

    Encrypted traffic and network intelligence

    Classification of encrypted traffic, IoT device and protocol identification from flow-level and protocol-level features, and separation of DoS from DDoS attacks using lightweight machine learning.

    Datasets and benchmarking

    Class imbalance and benchmarking in network security: comparative studies of SMOTE, bagging, chunking and GAN-based balancing, and construction of merged benchmark datasets for cloud and 5G network security.

    Earlier Research

    • Wireless mesh networks: analytical models of packet loss probability, medium access behaviour, interference and capacity in multi-hop wireless networks.
    • Cognitive radio networks: spectrum handoff, routing and spectrum decision, and channel assignment.
    • LTE: scheduling and resource allocation for service-class support.
    • Other contributions: PCB escape routing algorithms, an SNMP/MIB-based IoT control API, WiFi access point selection, and wearable-sensor-based human motion analysis (emotion recognition from gait, fall detection, Parkinson’s symptom identification).
