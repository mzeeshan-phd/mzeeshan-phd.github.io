My research applies artificial intelligence to network security. The aim is intrusion detection and prevention that is adaptive, efficient and deployable inside the network, and that closes the gap between results on research benchmarks and behaviour in operational networks.
{: .lead}

## Research Themes

### 1. Intrusion detection for IoT, cloud and industrial networks

**Problem.** IoT, cloud and industrial networks carry high-volume, heterogeneous traffic, and detection has to run on constrained devices and inside the network path.

**Contribution.** Protocol-based and feature-driven deep learning for intrusion detection on the UNSW-NB15 and BoT-IoT datasets, real-time SDN-based intrusion detection and prevention architectures, and IoT device identification. Ongoing work covers attention-driven deep learning for DDoS detection in cloud networks, lightweight hybrid autoencoders for ARP-based man-in-the-middle detection, LSTM-based autoencoders for IoT botnet and Industrial IoT intrusion detection, DNS tunneling detection, and adversarially hardened Android malware detection.

**Impact.** The two lead papers in this theme (IEEE Access 2022 and EURASIP JWCN 2021) have been cited 284 and 214 times.

### 2. Phishing detection and URL classification

**Problem.** Web-based attacks increasingly evade blacklists and signature filters, and adversaries now generate novel phishing URLs synthetically.

**Contribution.** Feature engineering, deep learning, deep reinforcement learning and transformer-based hybrid models for phishing and malicious URL detection, including zero-content URL classification and detection of synthetically generated phishing URLs.

**Impact.** Zero-content URL classification was published at ICACS 2026, and journal-level work on reinforcement learning and transformer-based detection is in preparation.

### 3. Encrypted traffic, network intelligence and benchmarking

**Problem.** Most traffic is now encrypted, and public security datasets are imbalanced and narrow, so models trained on them generalise poorly.

**Contribution.** Encrypted traffic classification with bidirectional LSTM and CNN architectures, IoT device and protocol identification from flow-level and protocol-level features, lightweight separation of DoS from DDoS attacks, comparative studies of data balancing techniques (SMOTE, bagging, chunking and GAN-based), and construction of merged benchmark datasets for cloud and 5G network security.

**Impact.** Encrypted traffic classification was published at AIIT 2025. The benchmark and data balancing studies are in preparation.

## Industry Research and Development

From 2021 to 2025 I was R&D consultant and Principal Investigator with Valyrian Systems Inc., USA, on a network security system that was deployed and remains in service. My work covered the system design, protocol identification from raw traffic, and AI-based attack identification. I also trained the engineers who joined the project.

This work keeps my academic research grounded in the practical constraints of operational networks.

## Directions for the Next Three Years

- Move from narrow, dataset-specific models towards generalised, multi-task security intelligence that works across heterogeneous networks.
- Validate detection models on real traffic captures and synthetic adversarial scenarios, beyond public benchmarks.
- Publish cloud-specific and 5G benchmark datasets with a consistent attack taxonomy.
- Secure Internet of Medical Things infrastructures for smart healthcare, under the MoHERI-funded project (2026 – 2028).
- Make detection robust to generated phishing content and adversarial inputs.
