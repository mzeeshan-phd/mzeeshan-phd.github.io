I work on artificial intelligence for network security. Over fifteen years of teaching and research in Oman, Pakistan and the USA, I have published 40 peer-reviewed papers, led industry-funded R&D that is deployed in production, and led programme accreditation and curriculum design.
{: .lead}

{{STATS}}

I am an Associate Professor in the Faculty of Computing and Information Technology at [Sohar University](https://www.su.edu.om/), Oman, and a Senior Member of the IEEE. Previously I was on the faculty of the [National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST)](https://seecs.nust.edu.pk/faculty/muhammad-zeeshan-3/), Islamabad, for nine years, where I continue to supervise postgraduate research. I received my Ph.D. from NUST in 2016, with part of the research carried out at Michigan State University, USA.

My group builds machine learning and deep learning systems for network intrusion detection and prevention, IoT and Industrial IoT security, phishing and malicious URL detection, and encrypted traffic analysis. More on the [Research](/research/) page.
