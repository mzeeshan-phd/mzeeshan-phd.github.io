I am an **Associate Professor** in the Faculty of Computing and Information Technology at [Sohar University](https://www.su.edu.om/), Oman, and a **Senior Member of the IEEE**. I joined Sohar University in September 2025. I am also an Associate Professor at the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (SEECS), [National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST)](https://seecs.nust.edu.pk/faculty/muhammad-zeeshan-3/), Islamabad, where I have been on the faculty since 2016 and continue to supervise postgraduate research.

My research applies artificial intelligence to cybersecurity and networking. My group builds machine learning and deep learning systems for network intrusion detection and prevention, with current work on IoT and Industrial IoT security, cloud network security, encrypted traffic classification, and phishing and malicious URL detection. Earlier in my career I worked on wireless mesh networks, cognitive radio networks and LTE resource allocation.

From August 2021 to June 2025 I was a Research and Development consultant with Valyrian Systems Inc., USA, where I led the design and deployment of AI-based intrusion detection and prevention, phishing detection, malware detection and URL classification systems for operational networks.

I received my Ph.D. in Information Technology from NUST in 2016, with part of the research carried out at Michigan State University, USA, as a visiting Research Fellow. I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering from the University of Engineering and Technology, Taxila (2009) and a B.S. in Computer Science from Allama Iqbal Open University, Islamabad (2005).

I have published 40 peer-reviewed papers, in venues including IEEE Access, IEEE Sensors Journal, Journal of the Franklin Institute, Journal of Information Security and Applications, and EURASIP Journal on Wireless Communications and Networking. As of November 2026 my Google Scholar profile records 1,280 citations, an h-index of 16 and an i10-index of 19.

## Research Interests

- AI-enabled cybersecurity and networking
- Network intrusion detection and prevention using machine learning and deep learning
- IoT and Industrial IoT security
- Phishing detection, URL classification and encrypted traffic analysis
- Wireless networks and computer networks
