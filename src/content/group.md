## What I Supervise

I supervise MS and Ph.D. research in artificial intelligence for network security:

- Intrusion detection and prevention for IoT, Industrial IoT, cloud and 5G networks
- Phishing detection and URL classification
- Encrypted traffic classification, and device and protocol identification
- Security of the Internet of Medical Things
- Datasets, benchmarking and class imbalance in network security

## Supervision Record

| Level | Completed | In progress |
|---|---|---|
| Ph.D. theses | 2 as committee member | 2 as supervisor |
| MS theses | 10 as supervisor; 35 as guidance committee member | 8 as supervisor |
| Undergraduate final year projects | 3 as supervisor; 5 as co-supervisor | 1 as supervisor |

Thesis topics supervised include deep learning-based malicious traffic detection in IoT networks, parameter-based threat detection, botnet detection, attack detection in MQTT-based IoT networks, application identification from encrypted traffic, phishing detection with ensemble learning, intelligent URL classification, and AI-powered cloud security.

## How to Contact Me

Email [muhammad.zeeshan@ieee.org](mailto:muhammad.zeeshan@ieee.org) with:

1. A short description of the problem you want to work on and why
2. Your CV and transcripts
3. Any research output, such as a thesis, paper or code

A background in computer networks and machine learning is the most useful preparation.
